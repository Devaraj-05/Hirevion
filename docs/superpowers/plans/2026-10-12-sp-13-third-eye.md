# SP-13 Third-eye, with Egress (D3)

**Goal:** PRD §52 (pairing with the five token properties), §53 (the third eye's transport), §63 (third-eye changes as events), §92 (the realtime path), and from SP-12 the Egress of the conversation audio and the third-eye video (§49, §85). Contract: `docs/api/contracts/third-eye.md`. ADR-0040.

## Decisions

- **Pairing** (`third_eye_pairings`, migration 0020): a 256-bit token, stored as its SHA-256, in the QR URL's fragment; 2 minutes to redeem; spent once; bound to the interview and its candidate; revocable. Issuing again revokes every live pairing of the interview and removes its phone from the room. One live pairing per interview (a partial unique index).
- **Redeem** finds the pairing without a tenant (a `SECURITY DEFINER` lookup owned by `hirevion_auth`, the 0019 pattern), then spends it under a row lock. It answers a 10-minute LiveKit token (identity `third-eye-{pairing}`, publish `camera` only, no subscribe, no data) and a **device secret**. `POST /third-eye/rejoin` exchanges the device secret for a fresh LiveKit token, so a phone that was locked longer than the token reconnects without a new QR code; revoking ends it.
- **Status is what LiveKit sees:** the third eye's camera `track_published` makes it `connected` (or `reconnected`), `track_unpublished` or `participant_left` `disconnected`. Each change publishes `THIRD_EYE_STATUS` and votes on SP-14's `third_eye` channel, which raises `THIRD_EYE_DISCONNECTED` after its 5 s debounce. Only the current pairing votes; a revoked phone that joins is removed.
- **A locked phone within 10 s:** the page unpublishes its camera on `visibilitychange`/`pagehide`, so LiveKit's webhook arrives at once; 5 s debounce + 1 s watcher poll.
- **`start`** needs a connected pairing when the third eye is required (the waiver still applies). The system check stores the server's view of the third eye in place of the desktop's.
- **Egress** (`hirevion_core.third_eye.egress`): room-composite audio only (candidate mic + agent voice, `mp4`) when the candidate joins a voice interview that records audio; track egress of each third-eye camera publication (`webm`). Each is a `recordings` row with `source = 'egress'`, tracks `conversation-audio-N` / `third-eye-N`, in §85's `audio/` and `third-eye/` folders. `egress_ended` queues `egress.ended`; the media worker moves the file into blob storage through an `EgressOutput` adapter (local: a shared volume; cloud: LiveKit writes to the blob store directly) and writes the timeline map, so playback lists it. The desktop-segment sweeps ignore egress rows.
- **Phone page:** pure modules (pairing API, platform guidance, session controller with injected LiveKit/camera/wake lock) under Vitest; React only renders.
