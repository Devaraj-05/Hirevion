# Recording policy

> **Draft for legal review**, not yet in force. Last updated: 2026-10-09.

## What is recorded, and when

Recording happens **only during the interview**, after you've seen the itemised notice and agreed. Before the interview you see exactly which of these your interview uses:

| Recording | What it captures | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Screen | Your screen while the interview app runs | To confirm you worked alone and to review coding answers |
| Camera | Video from your webcam | To confirm it's you and that you're working alone |
| Audio | Your microphone and the AI's voice | The interview conversation |
| Third eye | Video from a phone you pair, showing your desk and screen from the side | Only when the interview asks for one; you can ask for a waiver |

The transcript of both sides of the conversation is kept with the interview (see the [privacy policy](privacy.md)).

Nothing is recorded before you start the interview or after it ends. The app never records keystrokes.

## Where recordings are kept

- **Upload:** recordings go up in short, encrypted segments while the interview runs. Each segment is checked against its hash when it arrives, so a recording can't be altered unnoticed.
- **Storage:** they're kept in encrypted cloud storage in the organisation's own area, separate from every other organisation's.

## Who can watch them

- **The organisation's recruiting team** members whose role allows it, and the interview's reviewers.
- **Every playback** uses a link that works for a few minutes only, and is logged with who watched and when.
- **Hirevion staff** watch a recording only when the organisation asks for support with it, and that is logged too.

## How long they're kept

90 days by default, unless the organisation sets a different period (see the [data retention policy](data-retention.md)). After that they're deleted, including evidence clips cut from them, unless a legal hold applies.

## Your choices

- You can refuse a recording item. If the interview needs it, you can ask for an accommodation (for example, typed answers or a waiver of the phone camera) instead, and the organisation decides.
- You can ask for a copy of your recordings, or for their deletion (see the [deletion process](deletion.md)).
