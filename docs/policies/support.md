# Support and grievance contacts

> **Draft for legal review**, not yet in force. Last updated: 2026-10-09.

## Questions about your application or interview

Contact **the organisation you applied to**. Its support email is in your invitation and on the app's sign-in and interview screens. Ask it about:
- the job, your application or its outcome;
- rescheduling, accommodations and interview rules;
- a review of a decision by a person.

## Technical problems with the app

- **The interview app** shows a "Get help" link with diagnostic details you can choose to send.
- **Hirevion support:** support@hirevion.example (to be replaced with the production address), Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 IST.
- **During an interview:** if the app stops working, reopen it. The interview resumes where it stopped if you return within the time allowed.

## Privacy requests and complaints

- **The organisation** handles requests about your data first.
- **Hirevion's grievance officer** (DPDP Act, 2023): grievance@hirevion.example (to be replaced with the production address and the officer's name). We acknowledge within 3 working days and resolve within 30 days.
- **If you're not satisfied** with the answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India. Where the GDPR applies, you may complain to your local supervisory authority.

When you write, include the organisation's name and the email address you applied with. Never send your password.
