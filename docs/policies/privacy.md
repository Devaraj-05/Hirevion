# Privacy policy

> **Draft for legal review**, not yet in force. Last updated: 2026-10-09.

This policy explains how your personal data is handled when you apply for a job with an organisation that uses Hirevion, and when you take an AI interview on Hirevion's desktop app.

## Who is responsible

- **The organisation you applied to** decides why and how your data is used. It is the *data fiduciary* under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (and the controller under other laws such as the GDPR). Its name and support contact are in your invitation email and in the app.
- **Hirevion** runs the platform for that organisation and processes your data only on its instructions (the processor). Hirevion doesn't sell your data or use it for its own purposes.

## What we collect

| Category | Examples | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Identity and contact | Name, email, phone, location | Your resume; the organisation |
| Application | Resume, work history, skills, education, projects | Your resume (we keep a verified quote of where each fact came from) |
| Screening | How your profile matches the job, with the evidence used | Calculated by the platform |
| Account and device | Sign-in history, a device key, app version, operating system | The desktop app |
| Interview | Your spoken or typed answers, the transcript of both sides, timings | The interview |
| Recordings | Screen, camera, audio and, if used, a phone camera ("third eye") | The interview, after your consent |
| Coding activity | Code you write, runs and submissions | The interview's editor |
| System telemetry | Window focus, displays, applications that aren't allowed (never keystrokes) | The desktop app |
| Proctoring events | Signals such as "face not visible" or "second screen", with timestamps | The desktop app and the platform |

We don't ask for, and the screening never uses, your age, gender, religion, caste, marital status, health or similar characteristics. Government ID numbers that appear on a resume (Aadhaar, PAN) are masked before anything is stored.

## Why we use it

- **To consider your application** for the job you applied to: screening, shortlisting, interviewing and the hiring decision.
- **To run the interview fairly and securely:** confirming it's you, keeping the session secure and noticing possible rule breaks.
- **To keep records** the organisation is required to keep, and an audit trail of who did what.
- **To improve the service** only in aggregate and without identifying you (for example, how long interviews take).

The legal basis is your **consent**, which you give for each item before the interview and may withdraw. Where a law allows it, the organisation may also rely on its legitimate interest in recruiting, or on a legal obligation.

## The AI's role

- **Screening:** AI reads your resume and suggests how well it matches the job, citing evidence from your resume. People at the organisation review the ranking and make every decision.
- **The interview:** an AI interviewer asks questions, chooses follow-ups and keeps time. It never tells you a score.
- **Afterwards:** answers are evaluated against a rubric, each score with quotes from the transcript.

No decision to reject you is made by AI alone. A person reviews the results and decides. You can ask for a human review of any decision (see [support](support.md)).

## Who sees your data

- **The organisation's recruiting team**, limited by their role. For example, an interviewer sees only the interviews they review.
- **Hirevion's support staff**, only when needed to help the organisation or you, and every access is logged.
- **Service providers** acting for Hirevion under contract:
  - cloud hosting in India (Microsoft Azure, Central India);
  - AI model providers (OpenAI for language models, Deepgram for speech), who must not train on your data or keep it beyond processing;
  - email delivery.

## Transfers outside India

AI model providers may process data outside India. Transfers follow the DPDP Act and any restrictions the Indian government sets, with contractual safeguards. Where the GDPR applies, standard contractual clauses apply.

## How long we keep it

See the [data retention policy](data-retention.md). In short, recordings and proctoring events are kept for 90 days by default, and interview reports for one year, unless the organisation sets different periods.

## Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:
- access a summary of your data and how it's used;
- correct or update it;
- ask for it to be erased (see the [deletion process](deletion.md));
- withdraw consent (this may end your application);
- nominate someone to exercise your rights;
- raise a grievance.

Where the GDPR applies, you can also object, restrict processing and ask for a copy in a portable format. Contact the organisation, or Hirevion's grievance officer (see [support](support.md)). We answer within 30 days.

## Security

- **Encryption:** data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
- **Separation:** each organisation's data is kept apart from every other's at the database level.
- **Sign-in:** desktop sign-in is bound to your device's key.
- **Audit:** access is logged in a tamper-evident audit trail.

## Children

Hirevion is for job applicants aged 18 or over. If you're under 18, don't use it; contact the organisation instead.

## Changes

We'll update this policy when how we handle data changes. The interview's notice shows the current version before you agree to anything.
