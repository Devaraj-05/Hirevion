# Policies (drafts)

> **Draft for legal review.** These texts describe how Hirevion handles data as built. They aren't legal advice and mustn't be published to candidates until counsel for each customer organisation (the data controller) and for Hirevion (its processor) has reviewed them.

PRD §83 asks for six documents. The candidate portal (SP-07b) and the desktop app (SP-08) link to them, and the interview notice (SP-09) itemises what each interview collects.

| Document | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Privacy policy](privacy.md) | Who is responsible, what is collected and why, the AI's role, sharing, transfers, rights |
| [Recording policy](recording.md) | Screen, camera, audio and third-eye recording: when, where it's kept, who can watch |
| [Proctoring policy](proctoring.md) | The integrity signals collected, what they never decide, review and appeals |
| [Data retention policy](data-retention.md) | How long each kind of data is kept, and who sets it |
| [Data deletion process](deletion.md) | How a candidate asks for deletion, what happens, and what's kept |
| [Support and grievance contacts](support.md) | Where to ask for help, raise a concern or exercise a right |

**Conventions:**
- "The organisation" is the employer using Hirevion to hire; it decides why and how candidates' data is used (the data controller, or *data fiduciary* under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).
- "Hirevion" is the platform provider processing data on the organisation's behalf (the processor).
- Retention periods quoted are the product's defaults (PRD §84); each organisation may set its own (SP-22), and its own notice then applies.
- When a policy changes, its "Last updated" date changes, and the interview notice's version (`NOTICE_VERSION` in `hirevion_core.interview_session.briefing`) changes with it, so candidates agree to the current text.
