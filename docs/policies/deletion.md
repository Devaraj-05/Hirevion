# Data deletion process

> **Draft for legal review**, not yet in force. Last updated: 2026-10-09.

## Asking for deletion

You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time:
- **In the candidate portal:** go to Requests, then "Delete my data" (SP-07b).
- **By email:** write to the organisation's support contact named in your invitation, or to Hirevion's grievance officer (see [support](support.md)).

We confirm it's you before acting, usually by sending a code to the email address the organisation has for you. We never ask for passwords.

## What happens

1. **We acknowledge** your request within 3 working days and tell you who is handling it.
2. **The organisation reviews it.** It may keep some data if a law requires it or a legal claim is pending; it tells you what and why.
3. **Within 30 days** of your request, we delete or irreversibly anonymise:
   - your account and device registrations;
   - your resume, profile and screening results;
   - your interviews' recordings, transcripts, answers, evaluations and reports;
   - proctoring events and evidence clips.
4. **We confirm** in writing that it's done.

If you have an interview in progress or scheduled, deletion cancels it.

## What is kept

- **The audit log** keeps a minimal, tamper-evident record that the data existed and was deleted, with who asked and when, but not the data itself.
- **Backups** age out within 35 days of deletion and are never restored to bring deleted data back.
- **Legal holds:** data under one is deleted when the hold ends.
- **Aggregates:** statistics that can't identify you, such as how many interviews took place in a month, aren't affected.

## For organisations

Deletion requests appear in Hirevion's admin area. An organisation administrator approves or explains each refusal, and every step is audited. The same process runs when an organisation's retention period ends (see the [data retention policy](data-retention.md)).
